The house

Bảo Lộc's mist, a Vietnamese home's warmth.

Bảo Lộc sits high enough in Lâm Đồng that the clouds settle into the tea and coffee terraces most mornings, then burn off by ten. Our home was built for exactly that view — wide windows, a long balcony, and a garden that our family still tends by hand.

Stay with us the way you'd stay with family: help yourself to the garden, ask us where the good waterfalls are, and come down for breakfast whenever the mist lifts.
